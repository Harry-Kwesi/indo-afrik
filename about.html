Trade between makers and markets.

Indo-Afrik sources goods and moves them across borders, so buyers deal with one company from order to delivery.

We find the product, book the freight, clear customs and deliver to the door.

What we do.

Four jobs, handled by one team.

  • Sourcing

    We find suppliers for clothes, LPG machines, agricultural and medical equipment, furniture, shoes and lingerie, and check the goods before they ship.

  • Freight forwarding

    We choose the mode, by air, sea, rail, road or courier, and book the space your cargo needs.

  • Customs and documents

    We prepare the invoices, packing lists and certificates, and clear customs at both ends.

  • Delivery

    We carry the goods the last stretch to your port, warehouse or door.

Work with us.

Buying, selling or shipping? Tell us what you need and we will reply with a plan.

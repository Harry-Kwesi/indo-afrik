Trade between makers and markets

Indo-Afrik sources goods and moves them across borders, so buyers deal with one company from order to delivery.

We find the product, book the freight, clear customs and deliver to the door.

What we do

Four jobs, one team

You speak to one company for the whole journey, from the factory floor to your warehouse.

Sourcing

We find suppliers for all seven product lines and check the goods before they ship.

Freight forwarding

We choose the mode and book the space your cargo needs, by air, sea, rail, road or courier.

Customs and documents

We prepare invoices, packing lists and certificates, and clear customs at both ends.

Delivery

We carry the goods the last stretch, to your port, warehouse or door.

Trade flows both ways

Goods leave India for Africa and beyond, and come back the other way. We handle both directions.

Work with us

Buying, selling or shipping? Tell us what you need and we will reply with a plan.
